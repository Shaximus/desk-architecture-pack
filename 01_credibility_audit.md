# Credibility audit of the viral desk posts

Four posts, one weekend grammar.

## Pattern

20-40s motion graphic. Numbered agent pipeline. Specific P&L. Quote of the author's own guide. Cross-replies inside the same cluster. Optional bot-builder link.

Sharing those URLs trains the X recommendation graph to show you more of the same. That is farm targeting, not the market speaking to you.

### MAXdeg0 @MAXdeg0 / 2096642379872628884

Claim: 200 Grok Bots, context graph, one trading brain.
Quotes Ruuj @RuujSs article on alpha lifecycle, Deflated Sharpe, walk-forward, Research -> Risk -> Sizing -> Execution.
The Ruuj essay is the only serious object. MAX does not ship that code. Earlier posts used 350 and 300 bots. Grok Bot group chats hold 2-6 bots on one shared computer.

## Real underneath

- Grok Bot product, persistent cloud computer, named bots, skills, routines.
- Polymarket 5m/15m crypto binaries, official taker fee C * 0.07 * p * (1-p).
- Complete-set identity: matched Up+Down redeems $1. After two taker fees a 7c gross lock is about 3.6c if both legs fill.
- Public grind wallets turn millions of volume for mid-six-figures. That is a ~1% turnover edge, not a $60 lottery.

## Rejected

- Grok Bot group-chat as a millisecond CLOB sniper.
- 200 isolated Grok Bots on one account.
- Unsupervised wallet webhooks on the shared computer.
- Paid no-code trials attached to these videos.
- Copy-trading the named nicks.
